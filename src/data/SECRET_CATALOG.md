### Black Vein Station – secret camera room

- **POI:** Black Vein Station (`49:-70`; poi)
- **Type:** hidden_underwater_room
- **Confidence:** community-confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** The entrance is well below the main station and above you once you swim into the concrete tower.
- **Solution:** Follow the concrete tower down roughly four-fifths of its height. Swim into the open section and look upward for a small square opening leading to the hidden room.
- **Contents:** A comparatively rich hidden room; community calls it the camera room.
- **Conditions:**
  - `underwater_entry` – Dive down the concrete tower and enter the square opening from below.

### Central Warehouse Alpha – hidden map room

- **POI:** Central Warehouse Alpha (`66:22`; poi)
- **Type:** hidden_room
- **Confidence:** confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** The secret is in the upper office. One storage unit has a handle that looks far more useful than the others.
- **Solution:** Gain access to the upper office and use the yellow/orange handle on the wall locker/shelving unit. The unit slides aside and reveals the hidden map room.
- **Contents:** Hidden map room; discovering it completes The Warehouse quest and reveals the large-world map/lore scene.
- **Conditions:**
  - `security_level` – Reach the security-gated upper office; community reports describe this as Security Clearance 3 / roughly character level 15.
  - `movable_shelf` – Pull the yellow/orange handle on the wall storage unit.

### Mother Under the Sea

- **POI:** Sanctuary Dock 37 (`11:8`; poi)
- **Type:** hidden_landmark
- **Confidence:** confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** Close to where you first woke, one of the building's pipes continues far deeper than normal exploration suggests.
- **Solution:** Facing Sanctuary Dock 37's large main door from outside, follow the pipe attached to the building on the left down to roughly 200 m. The hidden Mother statue is at the bottom area; approaching it triggers the achievement.
- **Contents:** Hidden Mother statue and Steam achievement 'Mother Under the Sea'.
- **Conditions:**
  - `deep_dive` – Descend to roughly 200 m; high Pressure enhancement is required.
  - `pipe_route` – Follow the pipe left of the Sanctuary's large main door.
- **Known issues:**
  - The upper pipe section contains an accessible dead-end with scrap/memories; a different above-water curved section is not intended normal access.

### Sanctuary Dock 37 – ceiling catwalks

- **POI:** Sanctuary Dock 37 (`11:8`; poi)
- **Type:** hidden_path
- **Confidence:** confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** Look high above the basin near the facility-power balcony. Hazard paint marks a route that is easy to dismiss as scenery.
- **Solution:** Go to the balcony with the main facility power switch. Facing the switch, turn right and look up for the black/yellow hazard-painted ledge. Jump from the railing onto it. Midway along the catwalk, remove two vent covers and cross to the opposite catwalk.
- **Contents:** Scrap, Survival Dollars and Memories.
- **Conditions:**
  - `platforming` – Jump from the power-switch balcony railing to the hazard-painted ledge.
  - `removable_vent` – Remove two vent covers on the catwalk.

### The Fourth Chamber – statue vault

- **POI:** The Fourth Chamber (`109:-34`; poi)
- **Type:** environmental_puzzle
- **Confidence:** confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** Three scanned statues must be reconstructed in a specific historical order before the powered door will cooperate.
- **Solution:** At the hidden cave around 109:-34 (community thread uses 108:-34), place the replicated statues left-to-right as Augustus of Prima Porta, Moses, then Vulcan, God of the Forge. Connect power to open the locked door.
- **Contents:** Additional loot, QR-code message and access to the inner chamber; the cave also contains four painting samples.
- **Conditions:**
  - `replicated_objects` – Replicate and place three scanned statues.
  - `ordered_placement` – Left-to-right: Augustus of Prima Porta → Moses → Vulcan, God of the Forge.
  - `power_required` – Connect power after the statues are correctly placed.
- **Notes:**
  - Coordinate difference is only 1 map unit: current Wiki locations table says 109:-34; Steam sample thread says 108:-34.

### Secret of the Maze

- **POI:** The Maze (Transposium) (`111:22`; poi)
- **Type:** multi_trigger_puzzle
- **Confidence:** confirmed
- **Status:** accessible
- **Hint:** Three ordinary-looking objects in three numbered rooms control the hidden room. The three green indicators in room 037 show your progress.
- **Solution:** Activate the clock in the second room 090, the painting in room 026, and the hidden button behind boxes in room 034. The hidden room is associated with room 037 and the Steam achievement 'Secret of the Maze'.
- **Contents:** Hidden room / achievement: Secret of the Maze.
- **Conditions:**
  - `hidden_switch` – Activate clock in the second room 090.
  - `hidden_switch` – Activate painting in room 026.
  - `hidden_button` – Activate hidden button behind boxes in room 034.
- **Known issues:**
  - Players report cases where all three lights are green but the secret door remains closed.
  - The trigger objects can become non-interactive, making recovery difficult; save/reload does not always fix it.
